Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Coco & Brown

I chose the name COCO & BROWN because it represents fashion, confidence, and personal style. The name is simple, attractive, and easy for visitors to remember.

Possible domain: cocoandbrown.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide fashion and clothing information, outfit inspiration, and ideas for different occasions. Visitors will be able to explore clothing styles, seasonal fashion trends, accessories, and tips on how to put outfits together.

3. Visitor Scenarios

Visitors may have questions such as:

  • What outfits can I wear for a casual day out?
  • What are the latest fashion trends for this season?
  • How can I match accessories with my clothing?
  • What should I wear for a special occasion?

4. Color Scheme

5. Typography

The website will use two fonts to keep the design attractive and easy to read.

Georgia

Used for headings and section titles.

Arial

Used for paragraphs and general content.
